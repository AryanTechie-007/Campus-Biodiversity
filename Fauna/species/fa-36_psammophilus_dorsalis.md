## Zoological & Morphological Description

Medium-sized rock-dwelling lizard. Breeding males show a bright yellow-orange body with a black throat and flanks, while females and juveniles are brown with dark blotches.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer / Carnivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Insectivore that helps regulate ants, beetles and other ground insects.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Preys on insects and other small invertebrates.
  * Prey for birds of prey, snakes and mammals.
