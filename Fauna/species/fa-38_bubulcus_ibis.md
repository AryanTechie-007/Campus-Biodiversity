## Zoological & Morphological Description

Small, stocky white heron with a yellow bill; breeding birds carry buff plumes on the head, chest and back. Often forages around grazing animals and mowers.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer / Carnivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Eats grasshoppers, flies and other insects, and carries nutrients from feeding areas to roost trees.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Preys on insects, frogs and small reptiles found in lawns.
  * Roosts in groups in tall trees, sharing space with other birds.
