## Zoological & Morphological Description

Broad-bodied, dark-brown crab with a pair of pincers and four pairs of walking legs; moves sideways.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Omnivore / Detritivore (Trophic Level 2–3)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Scavenger that recycles dead organic matter back into the soil and water.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Feeds on detritus, plant matter and small invertebrates.
  * Prey for birds, snakes and mammals.
