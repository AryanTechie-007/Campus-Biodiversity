## Zoological & Morphological Description

Stocky toad with dry, warty brown skin, bony ridges on the head, and toxin-secreting glands behind the eyes.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer / Carnivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Eats slugs, ants, beetles and mosquitoes, and is a useful indicator of damp, healthy microhabitats.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Preys on insects and other small invertebrates.
  * Its skin toxin deters most predators; snakes are among the few that eat it.
