# Asian Koel (*Eudynamys scolopaceus*)

![Asian Koel](../images/eudynamys_scolopaceus.png)

## Taxonomy & Classification
- **ID**: FA-42
- **Common Name**: Asian Koel
- **Scientific Name**: *Eudynamys scolopaceus*
- **Family**: Cuculidae
- **Order**: Cuculiformes (Cuckoos)
- **Category**: Bird (Avifauna / Cuculidae)
- **Sub-Category**: Birds (Avifauna)

## Habitat & Occurrence
- **Location**: High Canopy Broadleaf Foliage & Neem Canopy
- **Habitat**: Dense tree canopy, neem groves, high foliage foliage branches
- **Survey Source**: Assignment 2 Survey (Team Asmath)
- **Contributor Team**: Team Asmath

## Ecological Role & Description
- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer & Frugivore-Insectivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecological Role**: Arboreal frugivorous and insectivorous cuckoo that consumes figs, berries, caterpillars, and tree insects. Brood parasite primarily laying eggs in House Crow nests.
- **Description**: Large, long-tailed cuckoo with striking sexual dimorphism. Males are glossy blue-black with a pale green bill and crimson eyes; females are dark brown spotted and barred with white.
