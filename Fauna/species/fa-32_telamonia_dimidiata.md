## Zoological & Morphological Description

Small, brightly patterned jumping spider with two dark stripes along the body. A day-active hunter that stalks and pounces on prey.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer / Carnivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Controls small insect populations without using a web.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Preys on flies, small bugs and other insects.
  * Prey for birds, lizards and larger spiders.
