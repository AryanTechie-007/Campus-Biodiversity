## Zoological & Morphological Description

Small, well-camouflaged frog with mottled brown-olive skin, a pointed snout and longitudinal skin ridges; jumps erratically when disturbed.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer / Carnivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Feeds on small insects and moves energy between aquatic and land habitats.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Preys on ants, flies and small beetles.
  * Prey for snakes, birds and larger frogs.
