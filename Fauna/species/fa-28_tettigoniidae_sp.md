## Zoological & Morphological Description

Green, leaf-like insect with very long antennae and folded wings that give excellent camouflage among foliage. Usually active at night.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Primary Consumer / Herbivore (Trophic Level 2)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Converts plant material into food for higher trophic levels.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Eats leaves and soft plant tissue.
  * Prey for birds, lizards, spiders and frogs.
