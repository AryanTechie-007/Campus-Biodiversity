## Zoological & Morphological Description

Stout, venomous snake with a triangular head and chain-like rows of dark, white-edged oval blotches along the body. Mainly nocturnal.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Level**: Tertiary Consumer / Carnivore (Trophic Level 4)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Predator that helps control rodent populations.
- **Campus Food Web Dynamics**:
  * Preys on rats, mice and other small vertebrates.
  * Few natural predators; young may be eaten by birds of prey and mongooses.
