# 🐾 Common Myna (*Acridotheres tristis*)

![Common Myna](../images/acridotheres_tristis.png)

## 📌 Taxonomy & Classification
- **ID**: FA-41
- **Common Name**: Common Myna
- **Scientific Name**: *Acridotheres tristis*
- **Family**: Sturnidae
- **Order**: Passeriformes (Perching Birds)
- **Category**: Bird (Avifauna / Sturnidae)
- **Sub-Category**: Birds (Avifauna)

## 📍 Habitat & Occurrence
- **Location**: Central Campus Grass Lawns & Dining Precincts
- **Habitat**: Open lawns, paved walkways, outdoor canteen perimeters
- **Survey Source**: Assignment 2 Survey (Team Asmath)
- **Contributor Team**: Team Asmath

## 🔬 Ecological Role & Description
- **Trophic Level**: Secondary Consumer & Omnivore (Trophic Level 3)
- **Ecological Role**: Highly adaptable urban passerine bird that feeds on ground insects, grasshoppers, seeds, and anthropogenic food scraps, regulating turf pest populations.
- **Description**: Medium-sized brown starling with a black head, distinctive bright yellow orbital patch behind the eye, yellow bill, yellow legs, and white wing patches visible in flight.
