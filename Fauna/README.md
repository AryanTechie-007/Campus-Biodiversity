# 🐾 Campus Fauna Inventory

> **Department of Environmental Science & Engineering (EVS)**  
> **Shiv Nadar University Chennai (SNU Chennai)**  
> **Total Documented Faunal Species:** 15 Species across 6 Taxonomic Guilds

---

## Overview

The campus zoological diversity comprises 15 distinct species spanning Arthropods (Insects & Myriapods), Molluscs, Crustaceans, Reptiles, Avifauna (Birds), and Mammals. These fauna occupy Trophic Levels 2 (Primary Consumers / Decomposers), Level 3 (Secondary Consumers / Insectivores), and Level 4 (Tertiary Consumers / Apex Scavengers & Predators).

## Key Conservation Highlight
- **Endemic Schedule I Species**: *Pachliopta hector* (Crimson Rose Butterfly) is granted Schedule I legal protection under India's Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, highlighting the high conservation and microhabitat value of SNU Chennai's flowering gardens.
- **National Bird of India**: *Pavo cristatus* (Indian Peafowl) regularly forages along the Dhanyas pathway and campus meadows.
