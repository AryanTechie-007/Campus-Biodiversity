## Botanical & Morphological Description

Glabrous succulent perennial herb noted for thick, scalloped fleshy leaves capable of vegetative reproduction from leaf crenations. Known widely as the 'Miracle Leaf' or 'Air Plant'.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Role**: Primary Producer (Trophic Level 1)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Crassulacean acid metabolism (CAM) succulent bearing crenate fleshy leaves that produce adventitious plantlets along leaf margins (vegetative propagation). Provides medicinal phytochemicals and drought-resilient microhabitat.
- **Inter-species Interactions**:
  - Provides vital floral rewards (nectar, pollen) or structural microhabitats for campus biodiversity.
  - Supports pollinators (butterflies, bees, flower-visitors) and detritivore nutrient recycling.
  - Forms an integral component of the campus green spaces.
