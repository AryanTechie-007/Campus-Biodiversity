## Botanical & Morphological Description

Erect perennial subshrub with deeply veined, dark green serrated leaves. Exhibits mutualistic interactions with native ants through nectar-secreting glands on petioles.

---

## Ecological Role & Campus Interactions

- **Trophic Role**: Primary Producer (Autotroph - Trophic Level 1)
- **Ecosystem Service**: Serrated leaves with paired extrafloral nectaries at leaf bases that attract ants for biological protection; bright yellow diurnal flowers sustain diverse butterflies and bees.
- **Inter-species Interactions**:
  - Sustains insect pollinators (bees, butterflies, sphinx moths) with nectar and floral resources.
  - Generates structural biomass, microhabitats, and leaf litter for detritivores (millipedes, snails).
  - Provides seeds/drupes and sheltered resting canopy for campus avifauna and primates.
